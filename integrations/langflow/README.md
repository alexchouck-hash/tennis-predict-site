# Langflow Custom Component: iPredictSport Tennis & Market Alpha

Integrate live ATP/WTA machine learning predictions into your visual AI workflows with [Langflow](https://github.com/langflow-ai/langflow).

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Visual Pipeline Drag-and-Drop**: Connect tennis prediction market edges directly to LangChain agents, OpenAI LLMs, or Telegram alert nodes.
- **Audited Predictions**: Live feed from `https://ipredictsport.com/predictions.json` (free & unauthenticated).
- **Quarter-Kelly Sizing**: Pre-calculated mathematical risk stakes.

---

## 🚀 How to Add to Langflow

1. Open your Langflow Canvas.
2. Click **Custom Component** under the Components sidebar.
3. Paste the contents of [`ipredict_component.py`](ipredict_component.py).
4. Connect the output to a Chat Output, Agent, or Webhook node!
